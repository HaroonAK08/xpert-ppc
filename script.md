# Xpert PPC Case Studies Video Script

**Format:** Founder introduction  
**Length:** 60–75 seconds  
**Speaker:** Umer Khan, Senior PPC Specialist / Account Manager  
**Placement:** Case studies page, beside the hero headline  
**Primary goal:** Build trust quickly, then move the visitor to the case studies or free-audit CTA.

## Final spoken script

### Hook — 0:00–0:08

> If you’re spending money on ads and you’re not sure whether it’s really working, this page is for you.

### Who I am — 0:08–0:20

> I’m Umer Khan from Xpert PPC. I’ve spent more than 20 years working in data-driven performance marketing, managing Google, Meta and other paid campaigns for eCommerce brands, law firms and local businesses.

### The proof — 0:20–0:44

> Here are a few examples of what that work looks like. One established eCommerce brand improved ROAS from 150% to 600% in one year. A law firm reduced cost per acquisition from $162 to $126 while saving around $72,000 in ad spend. And a locksmith and plumbing business generated 1,059 conversions in 90 days, at under $25 each.

### How we work — 0:44–1:03

> There are no shortcuts or magic tricks. We find where budget is being wasted, fix the tracking so leads and sales are counted properly, and then put more budget behind what is working. The numbers on this page come from real campaign reporting, with the measurement limits explained clearly.

### Call to action — 1:03–1:15

> Choose your industry below and look through the case studies. If you want us to review your account, request a free PPC audit. We’ll show you where the biggest opportunities are and what to fix first.

## Recording instructions

- Record horizontally in 1080p, while leaving enough space for a vertical crop for Reels, Shorts and LinkedIn.
- Face a window or soft light. Do not place the window behind you.
- Use a wired lavalier microphone or wired earphones in a quiet room. Clear sound matters more than a cinematic background.
- Keep the camera at eye level and look into the lens, not at the phone screen.
- Use a plain, tidy background such as your desk or a clean office wall.
- Speak naturally. Small pauses and a slightly imperfect delivery feel more credible than an over-produced performance.
- Record three takes: one calm, one more energetic, and one conversational. Use the most natural take.

## Editing instructions

- Remove long pauses, repeated words and obvious mistakes, but do not make the delivery unnaturally fast.
- Add burned-in subtitles because many visitors watch without sound.
- Keep background music very quiet or omit it entirely. The voice must remain easy to understand.
- Use dashboard overlays only while the relevant result is being spoken.
- Show the case-study page or a blurred report, not stock footage.
- End on the same orange “Get my free audit” CTA used on the page.

## Thumbnail

Use Umer’s face with one clear number:

> **600% ROAS**  
> Real account. Real result.

Keep the thumbnail simple: face, one number, dark navy background, and the orange CTA accent. Do not put three or four metrics on the thumbnail.

## Performance-safe website placement

Do not autoplay the video or load a YouTube/Vimeo player immediately. The case studies page already needs to stay fast on mobile.

Use this flow:

1. Show a compressed poster image with Umer’s face and a play button.
2. Load the video player only after the visitor clicks.
3. Keep the poster image properly sized and served as WebP or AVIF.
4. Provide a text transcript below or beside the video for accessibility and SEO.
5. Track play clicks as an analytics event, but do not block the page on the analytics request.

## Accuracy rules

- Keep the figures exactly as shown in the approved case-study content and dashboard evidence.
- Do not claim that every result came from one channel if the case study combines Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO or management-reported figures.
- Do not say “guaranteed,” “best agency,” or “we can double your revenue.”
- If a figure is estimated, segment-specific or from a different reporting period, say so in the case-study note rather than hiding the limitation.
- Update the script before filming if any approved case-study number changes.

## Optional follow-up videos

### Client testimonial — 30–45 seconds

Ask the client:

1. What problem were you dealing with before Xpert PPC?
2. What changed after the work began?
3. What result or improvement mattered most to your business?
4. What would you say to another business owner considering Xpert PPC?

### Screen walkthrough — 2–3 minutes

Use this for the “See how we run your ad spend” section:

1. Explain the account and reporting period.
2. Show where budget was spent.
3. Explain what counts as a lead, sale or conversion.
4. Show the main result and its limitations.
5. Walk through the weekly checks and the next optimization.

The founder video should remain short. The screen walkthrough is the place for detail.
